# Paper Branch Contract

`main` is the factual research trunk.  It retains the generalized-Suwako
mathematical core, verified prior-art boundary, and the complete `raw/`
provenance archive.  It deliberately contains no manuscript outline,
submission gate, application plan, or experimental checklist.

Venue branches are curated projections rather than copies of this directory:

- `math-comp` carries the theorem-first manuscript, its classical
  computational-algebra evaluation plan, and only the raw records useful to
  that draft.
- `eurocrypt` carries the cryptographic and reversible-circuit manuscript
  plans and their associated research tracks.

Import confirmed mathematical or prior-art corrections from `main` by
selective cherry-pick.  Do not merge all paper notes wholesale, because that
would reintroduce out-of-scope material.
